# Edumise BD — user guide (English)

Short task guides per role. Screenshots are from the demo school; your school's name, colours and data differ. Bangla version: `user-guide-bn.md`.

**Signing in:** open the school website, press **Login** (top right). Staff sign in with their email, guardians with their mobile number, students with their student ID. The first time, you must choose your own password. Forgot it? **Forgot password** emails a link to staff; guardians and students ask the office for a new one-time password.

![Sign-in page](guide/login-en.png)

The language switch (**বাংলা / English**) in the top bar changes the whole site and dashboard.

## Admin (office)

![Admin home](guide/admin-home-en.png)

**School settings** — name, address, phone, logo, colours, EIIN, head teacher's message. Everything on the website and on printed documents comes from here. Save, and the site changes at once.

![School settings](guide/admin-settings-en.png)

**Website:** Notices, News, Events, Gallery, Downloads and Pages. Each has an English and a Bangla tab; leave Bangla empty and the English text is shown. A draft is invisible until **Published** is ticked. Notices have a category and an audience (public, staff only, or one class). Teachers can write notice drafts; only the office publishes them.

![New notice](guide/admin-notice-en.png)

**Admissions:** online applications arrive in the queue. Open one, see the documents, schedule the admission test, enter the marks, and **Approve** into a section. Approval creates the student, the guardian and the guardian's login, and shows the one-time password once: print it or write it down for the family.

![Admissions queue](guide/admin-admissions-en.png)

**Students:** search, add, edit, link guardians, upload documents, print the ID card, create student or guardian logins, switch a login off. **Import** adds many students from a CSV file (download the template first). At the end of the year, **Promotion** moves a class into the next session.

![Students](guide/admin-students-en.png)

**Staff:** profiles (public and private details), designations, teaching assignments (subject per section) and logins with one-time passwords. Private details (NID, birth date, address, personal phone) never appear on the website.

![Staff](guide/admin-staff-en.png)

**Academic setup:** sessions (one is current), classes, sections with class teachers, groups, subjects and which class takes which subject.

**Exams:** create the exam, choose its classes and the full and pass marks per subject, move it to **Marks entry** so teachers can enter marks, then **Review**, **Process results** and **Publish**. Published results appear in the family portal and the public result search. Tabulation sheets and mark sheets print or download as PDF.

![Exams](guide/admin-exams-en.png)

**Routine:** periods and working days, the class routine builder (clashes of a teacher or room are blocked), the exam schedule and the holiday calendar.

![Routine builder](guide/admin-routine-en.png)

**Certificates:** testimonial, transfer or character certificate for a student, with a reference number and QR code that anyone can verify on the website. A revoked certificate shows as revoked.

![Certificates](guide/admin-certificates-en.png)

**Fees → Waivers** need an admin's approval, and only an admin can cancel a payment or switch online payment on.

## Teacher

**Attendance:** pick your section and the date (today by default; you can correct the last 3 days). Everyone starts as Present; tap a student to change to Absent, Late or Leave. **Save**. Works on a phone.

![Take attendance](guide/teacher-attendance-en.png)

**Marks:** pick the exam, your section and subject. Type the marks per part (CQ, MCQ, practical) or tick **Absent**; everything saves as you type. When all marks are in, **Submit & lock**. Only the office can unlock. Marks can also be imported from an Excel file made from the template.

![Marks entry](guide/teacher-marks-en.png)

**Notices:** write a draft; the office publishes it. **My routine** shows your own timetable.

![Notice draft](guide/teacher-notice-en.png)

## Accountant

![Accountant home](guide/accountant-home-en.png)

**Fee setup:** fee heads, the fee structure per class (and group), waivers (an admin approves them), the late fine and default due days.

**Make invoices:** choose a month (all monthly fees) or a one-time fee, the classes, and **Preview**: it says how many invoices and the total, and warns if any waiver is still waiting for approval. **Make** them. Running it again makes nothing twice.

![Make invoices](guide/accountant-generate-en.png)

**Collect:** find the student by ID, roll or name. Tick the items being paid, enter the amount (part payments are fine), the method and the transaction ID for bKash, Nagad or Rocket, then **Save and print receipt**. The late fine, if any, is added at this moment. The receipt shows the amount in words in English and Bangla and a QR code; print it or download the PDF.

![Collect fees](guide/accountant-collect-en.png)

**Reports:** daily collection (by method and by receiver), dues by class with the guardian's phone, income by fee for a date range, and a student's ledger. Each downloads as Excel or PDF.

![Dues report](guide/accountant-dues-en.png)

## Guardian

**My children** lists each child with links to their attendance calendar, results and fees.

![My children](guide/guardian-children-en.png)

**Attendance:** a calendar with the month's percentage.

![Attendance calendar](guide/guardian-attendance-en.png)

**Results:** published exams; open one for the mark sheet, which prints.

![Results](guide/guardian-results-en.png)

**Fees:** what is due now, every bill, and every receipt (open one to print or download it). When the school has switched online payment on, **Pay now** pays everything due through the gateway.

![Fees](guide/guardian-fees-en.png)

## Student

Signs in with the student ID. **My profile** shows the same attendance, results and fees pages as the guardian's, for the student's own record.
